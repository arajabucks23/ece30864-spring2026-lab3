CSP-17: Pros and Cons of Agile Development

Pros:
Agile allows teams to pivot quickly and adjust to changing requirements and feedback, whereas non-Agile approaches are more fixed once plans are set. Working software is delivered in small, frequent increments through sprints, allowing earlier releases and faster feedback instead of waiting until the final product is completed.

Cons:
Because requirements are not fixed upfront, there is a higher risk of scope creep if the project is poorly managed. Agile can also make it harder to accurately predict the final delivery date or total project cost at the beginning of the project.
